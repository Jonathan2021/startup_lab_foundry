Status: open. Affects venture `v-coopain` (HOLD since the 2026-10-09 portfolio review).
Nothing here authorises sending messages; an agent drafts the guide, you talk to people.

The finished local MVP needs no more features before demand is tested. The unchecked
risk is that many employer referral policies require the referrer to know the
candidate, which removes the referrer's motive for platform strangers.

Gate: five short conversations with employees at different French employers (friends,
former Amaris colleagues, family). **Pass** if at least 3 of 5 say their written
referral policy allows referring someone met through a platform and they would review
two or more requests a month. **Stop** if few say yes or policies generally require
knowing the candidate. Package C02 prepares the interview guide and a policy/competitor
desk pack (Basile by Hellowork, Keycoopt, Coop-Time, BeCoopt, Blind/Glassdoor forums).
