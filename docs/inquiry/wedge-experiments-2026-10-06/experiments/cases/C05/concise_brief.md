# C05: Pricing with a biased waitlist

EXPLICITLY SYNTHETIC.

Scope: Solo-consultant document checker; hypothetical prices only.
Scope revision: 3; question revision: 2.
Goal: Choose a sustainable paid offer for a narrowly defined customer job.
Question: Does the existing evidence justify a 19 euro monthly price and a paid launch?

Branch criteria:
- continue: Qualified buyers make real repeat purchases or equivalent bounded paid commitments covering variable cost.
- narrow: A subset has a clear recurring job and unit economics but broader audience does not.
- stop: Even qualified paid use fails the chosen margin/retention condition after the agreed test.
- unknown: Only interest, stated preference, or insufficient usage-cost evidence exists.

## Curated source brief

- C05-E1 [C05; r1; links: none]: Twenty-five friends joined a free waitlist after seeing a 19 euro mock price. No payment method, commitment or actual charge was collected.
- C05-E2 [C05; r1; links: clarifies C05-E1]: The first analysis called the waitlist '25 paying customers'; a correction states they are only free signups.
- C05-E3 [C05; r1; links: none]: Five usage examples produced variable inference/support costs ranging from 2 to 28 euros per user-month. The examples are too few to estimate the tail reliably.
- C05-E4 [C05; r1; links: none]: Two experienced consultants report a recurring monthly checking job, but neither has tried the product or agreed to pay.
- C05-E5 [C05; r1; links: none]: No real checkout, paid launch or spending was authorized. A reversible offer mockup and explicit test proposal are allowed.
