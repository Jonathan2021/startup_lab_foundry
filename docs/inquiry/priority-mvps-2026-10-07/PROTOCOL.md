## Bounded probes and gates

- Consult retained evidence, user corrections and official/current competitor and
  technical sources. Record URLs, access date, relevant claims and limitations.
- Volleyball: deterministic synthetic outcomes exercise team swaps, equal teams,
  expected/upset outcomes and replay after correction. This checks an MVP rating
  rule, not identification of individual skill or player adoption.
- Coopain: run relevant existing tests in an isolated environment/database, after
  reading fixtures; no email, production DB, storage account or provider calls.
  Preserve checkout, untracked data and history when moving it into Startup Lab.
- Routes: inspect real routing/profile/loop APIs and licenses; test a small public
  route request if allowed without credentials, plus synthetic Pareto filtering.
  Never label proxies as measured beauty, safety or calibrated motorcycle ETA.
- Crous: fetch a public official directory, verify reuse/license, counts, IDs and
  Cuvier/Châtelet; exercise synthetic report expiry, deduplication and disagreement.
- Coaching: small synthetic availability cases must fit duration/equipment/court
  constraints. Planning does not validate physical advice or automatic video accuracy.
- Foundry: isolated-store agent contract replay includes claims, conflicting workers,
  source changes, result review, restart and actionable feedback capture; then record
  live venture findings and next work with explicit identities and additive history.

A GO means bounded implementation is justified for a local/private pilot. It does
not mean demand, legal clearance, profitability, deployment or automatic execution.
Separate build prerequisites, first-user pilot gates and public-release gates.
A source/data-access blocker narrows scope when a useful MVP remains possible.
Paid APIs, hosting, sending, public publishing and training are outside this run.
Tests stop on unexpected external side effects. Synthetic observations stay labeled.
