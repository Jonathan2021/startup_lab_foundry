Frozen 2026-10-04 before runs. Inputs and initial dirty-code/status manifest:
ignored `.local/handoff-2026-10-04/input-manifest.json`. Canonical database remains
local; Hindsight is unnecessary and untested. Original campaign findings consulted
through the handoff's source register and existing venture evidence.

Route hypothesis: changed geometry explains at least half of the reported 89-minute
ETA difference under the Liberty Rider implied mean speed. Alternative: speed-model
or route-interpretation assumptions dominate. Compute haversine segment lengths,
start/end alignment, directed point-to-segment distances at 200 m samples, 25/50/100 m
bands, 95th percentile and largest divergence sections. Never bridge segments.
Estimate simplification error by downsampling the denser route to ~479 points.
Controls: identical, collinear sparse, detour and disjoint segments. Support requires
at least 44.5 minutes equivalent extra distance, weaken if materially below it,
otherwise inconclusive. No timestamp-based accuracy claim. Stop after one reproducible
pair analysis; no uploads/routing APIs. Exact-location SVG/HTML and data stay ignored.

Coopain: inspect reference-only non-secret selected assets and fixtures. Run three
representative suites in its own environment with memory SQLite/fake email after
checking fixture dependencies. One diagnosed environment blocker stops execution;
no service setup spiral. Matrix separates docs/code/mock-tests/observed UI. No real
SMTP, storage or identity provider. Inspect stable-ID compatibility placeholder.

Sports A: small private ranking/tournament. B: rotating attendance/roles, log result
and repeat. Public incumbent workflow attempt; account gates are recorded. Synthetic
12-person volleyball brief is illustrative, not the user's sport. Provisional
threshold setup <=10 min, result entry <=30 s; include late arrival, newcomer,
correction and returning participant. Two voluntary real sessions are the next
utility gate; no personal-use results or willingness to pay can be fabricated.

Physical: three task-relevant dataset sources, about 45 minutes. Require labeled
correct/incorrect performance of relevant physical work, license and failure
coverage. Action recognition alone fails suitability. No benchmark with unsuitable
labels and no quality-assurance claim. Receipt: clearly fictional bounded example;
four draft review invitations, no send. Real receiver decision is the next gate.
