## Method and bounded criteria

- Screen every idea at the description/category level; deeply inspect a shortlist
  spanning developer/research tools, a personal-use workflow, and vertical tools.
  Do not claim a complete market audit of 238 ideas.
- Prefer current official product documentation and distinguish vendor capability
  claims from independent evidence of pain, market size, or willingness to pay.
- Assess access to users/data, recurrence and cost of the problem, incumbent plus
  ordinary-script baseline, achievable scope, likely operating burden, and
  learning fit. Unsupported financial forecasts do not improve priority.
- A candidate may receive **trial** status when usable inputs and a bounded
  comparison exist. **Build** status requires observed workflow pain and a
  material advantage over the baseline. **Commercial expansion** also requires
  independent user/adoption evidence; this research cannot award it from websites.
- Stop broadening the search after Foundry, Agent EvalOps, and approximately eight
  materially different candidate directions have been compared. State unresolved
  customer/data access instead of substituting more speculative rankings.
- Use the existing Foundry CLI against a dedicated local database to retain this
  inquiry; inspect existing state first and preserve it. Keep a sanitized,
  portable record of inputs, evidence, assessments, decisions, and record IDs.
- No paid model calls, customer outreach, publication, cloud provisioning, or
  automatic external actions. No complete learner-owned technical solution.
- Revise current guidance and future high-level learning outcomes explicitly.
  Archive obsolete requirements with attribution; do not mark cancelled work
  passed or completed. Keep one active slice and preserve accepted regressions.
