## Assessment and decision

The generic “free collaborative restaurant queue app” is not a new wedge on the available evidence. Both institutional sensor-backed and community-based substitutes exist; an actual regional CROUS deployment is confirmed by the operator's publication. Keep the venture at **investigation / campus gap unverified**, not build-ready or invalidated. Assess differentiation as weak provisionally, with explicit counterevidence. Customer access, distribution, buyer, revenue model and live-data feasibility remain unknown. A free student product is a pricing intention, not proof of a viable venture or identified institutional buyer. No defensible complete score follows from this desk study alone.

Possible narrower hypothesis: a specific unserved restaurant cluster needs trustworthy walk-plus-wait arrival choices across routes/queue classes that existing tools fail to support. This is a hypothesis, not a discovered gap: restaurant occupancy already overlaps; student/staff queues matter only where these actually exist and diners are eligible. Current waiting time at departure differs from waiting time after a ten-minute walk. Rank alternatives only when opening/access/queue-class constraints and forecast-at-arrival uncertainty are known.

Cold start is the central operational risk. Static catalog/menu data enable a useful directory but cannot supply live labels. A crowdsourced completed-wait report arrives after its wait began; it can already be stale when received. Sparse lunch reporting, volunteer selection, holiday timetables, changing serving speed, abuse, and separate lines confound predictions. Queue length and seats are different observations; avoid deriving available seats from waiting time. Prefer manual venue selection and aggregate self-reported durations for a trial; exact student location histories and photos are unnecessary. API mentioned in marketing is not proof of a free public licensed feed. A partnership route remains an option only after access/terms are verified and outreach specifically approved.

## Next bounded experiment (proposed; not executed)

Gate 0: user names one accessible campus, one primary restaurant plus a walkable alternative, their eligible queue type, and whether they or existing willing contacts can record their own lunch visits. If access is unavailable, retain a specific blocked human-input request and do not build a nationwide app.

Phase A, at most three lunch periods: for the named locations, manually inspect the actual Affluences and CrousRadar interfaces (no reporting/upload needed), record timestamp, coverage, prediction availability, freshness if exposed, seats and queue distinctions. Match to a few own timed visits (queue join to defined endpoint: food paid/ready; record seat search separately). If existing tools are accurate enough for the actual decision, recommend using them or investigate a narrower gap. Do not call absent extracted website results proof of absent mobile coverage.

Phase B only if a gap survives: five lunch days, two venues, target at least 15 voluntary own-journey observations across three arrival bands, using a local table/form mock rather than released software. Record venue, queue type, join time, served/paid time, coarse seat availability, walking duration, prediction/abstention and its issuance time. No personal identity/location trail required. Volunteers must agree; no recruitment messages are authorized by this plan.

Freeze rules before collecting: last matched report no older than 15 minutes as the simplest live baseline; older/missing evidence returns “unknown.” Compare an incumbent if available plus a simple historical time-band baseline fitted only to earlier days. Split chronologically (days 1–3 exploration, 4–5 check); never evaluate on the records used to fit the prediction. Evaluate wait prediction at arrival, separately from walk duration. Record prediction MAE, coverage/abstention, fresh-report coverage, and whether suggested alternative could have saved at least five minutes after walking. Counterfactual savings stay estimates unless matched observations at both options exist.

Illustrative screening gate to accept/revise before fieldwork: at least 15 total observations and six held-out observations; fresh reporting for at least 70% of scheduled checks; held-out wait MAE no more than five minutes; a useful improvement over the incumbent or historical baseline on at least three measured decisions; at least two contributors willing to repeat without individual daily reminders. Failure/inadequate sample means revise or stop/inconclusive, not launch. These small counts are a feasibility screen, not statistical validation. Log whether improved coverage depends entirely on researcher effort. Continue only if both a local unmet need and a plausible repeatable data supply appear; study payer/distribution next, then authorize a very small prototype.
