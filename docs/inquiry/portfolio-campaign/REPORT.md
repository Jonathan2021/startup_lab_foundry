## The decision

Keep Foundry as an internal tool being tested through real investigations. Keep
Agent EvalOps as a deferred competition/test case. Do not build a generic trace,
human-feedback or evaluation platform to fill the learning schedule: the MLflow
trial demonstrated much of that plumbing already works.

N008 now has a concrete first-party problem: shortening a scenic ride to a time
budget while retaining valued sections and understanding GPX/ETA differences.
It is the next focused software investigation. Close competitor claims weaken
the case for a general scenic planner. Physical-task verification and portable
skill evidence remain access-held hypotheses, not three ready-to-build ventures.
No product learning slice is active and G002 is not a preselected replacement.

## Coverage, including a correction

All **238 workbook entries and nine new bullets** have stable IDs, retained source
text, a disposition and a next falsifying step across 37 investigation groups.
The attachments are unchanged. The current register has **85 sources and 187
scoped references**: **128 entries** have a comparator, component or adjacent
reference; **119** have an early scope/access/feasibility gate and still lack a
verified exact comparator. References do not imply a whole proposed job is solved.

This is not 247 completed market studies or hands-on comparisons. No candidate
is commercial-build qualified. The original workbook scores are provenance, not
current financial forecasts or learned success probabilities.

The first pass overattached category sources (including unrelated health and
visual-search analogues). A relevance audit removed those links and researched
closer alternatives. [Revision 1](revisions/r1/) preserves the original 45 sources
and 317 broad links used in T3/T6; [revision 2](revisions/r2/) preserves the audit
before the rider case. Current idea-level links are authoritative; group membership
does not inherit evidence. Lower coverage is an honest correction, not a decline
in the ideas themselves. The calibration rules now require claim-level relevance.

markets. Duplicate concepts and cross-idea links do not count as independent
customer demand. The [full register](REGISTER.md) records the other dispositions

not a claim the business can never work; further research should change a real
decision rather than produce more generic descriptions.

## Foundry tested through this work

The campaign's 247 first-pass tasks and their evidence, assessments and decisions
were recorded through existing Foundry application operations in an ignored local
database. [Portable operation log and snapshot](foundry-recording/) retain 1,514
successful operations, 249 assumptions/assessments/decisions, 253 evidence records,
253 work items, two experiments and 12 artifacts. Completed tasks mean initial
triage done, not commercial diligence done. A separate `v-route-repair` discovery
venture records the concrete N008 case and counterevidence in the same database.

One recurring defect justified a runtime change: the earlier inquiry and T1/T2
stored experiment criteria, but `venture show` omitted them. It now reconstructs
a deterministic, venture-scoped experiment collection. The isolation/protocol
test failed first and passed after the fix; no schema change or new service.
See [the product ADR](../../adr/0008-reconstruct-experiment-protocols.md).

The source register and exact dependency exercise remain campaign support, not a
new generic framework. Experiment lifecycle status still says planned after work
completion: evidence records contain results, and this distinct status-model
friction is documented for later specification rather than silently patched.
Hindsight remains optional, uninstalled and unbenchmarked. No shared library has
been justified across independent Foundry and ARC3 workflows.

## Commercial form and learning

Use an incumbent, contribute a small improvement, or abandon a premise when that
is the useful outcome. For a reproduced software gap, compare a small open-source
integration and service-assisted use before hosted SaaS. An open license can
support adoption and a portfolio; monetization still requires a buyer for a
specific outcome, operation or support. No license, price or publication is chosen.

The revised [ADR-0009](../../../../docs/adr/0009-agent-owned-portfolio-discovery.md)
keeps discovery agent-owned and both 005 variants inactive/unpassed. Future skill
objectives remain available but will attach to warranted work; this campaign earns
no learner certification credit. A working incumbent comparison can itself be
useful learning without committing to a weak startup.

The remaining maturity gap is explicit: one stronger software problem to test,
two hypotheses needing external access, and no demonstrated paying/repeating
adopter. Further web research cannot substitute for those observations. We have
completed the first allocation and technical counterchecks, not the requested
ultimate milestone of a few commercially supported ventures.
